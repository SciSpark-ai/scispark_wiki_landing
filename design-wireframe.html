A few things
to know.

Do I need to install anything?

You can start with the hosted beta in your browser. Local installation is also available if you prefer to keep a vault on your computer.

Can I use my own AI provider?

Yes. SciSpark includes a starter AI allowance, and you can also bring your own API key. Check your account for usage details.

How do papers become connected knowledge?

Save useful reading into your wiki, where papers, concepts, and notes link together. Sparky helps you ask questions and develop ideas from that context.

Is there a mobile app?

A mobile app is planned.
